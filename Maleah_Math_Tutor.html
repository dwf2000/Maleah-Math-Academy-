Skill #1: Converting Units

What is Unit Conversion?

Unit conversion is changing from one type of measurement to another, like inches to feet or ounces to pounds.

The Secret: Multiplication, Not Division!

Think of it like this: If you have bigger pieces, you need fewer of them. So you multiply by a fraction less than 1.

Example: Inches to Feet

inches × (1 foot / 12 inches) = feet
Step 1

Remember: 1 foot = 12 inches

Step 2

To go from SMALL (inches) to BIG (feet), multiply by 1/12

Why? Because 12 inches makes only 1 foot, so each inch is only 1/12 of a foot!

Step 3

Example: 5/7 inches = ?

5/7 × 1/12 = (5×1)/(7×12) = 5/84 feet

Common Conversions to Remember 📏

From To Multiply By
Inches Feet 1/12
Ounces Pounds 1/16
Feet Inches 12
Pounds Ounces 16

Skill #2: Perimeter

What is Perimeter?

Perimeter is the distance around the outside of a shape. Imagine walking around a fence - that's the perimeter!

Perimeter of Square = 4 × side

Why? A square has 4 equal sides, so you add side + side + side + side = 4 × side

Example:

Square with sides of 2/3 inches

Perimeter = 4 × 2/3 = 8/3 inches (which equals 2 2/3 inches)

Skill #3: Speed/Rate Problems

The Speed Formula

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

Think of it this way: How much distance do you cover in one unit of time?

Example:

A train travels x miles in 3/4 hour

Step 1

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

Step 2

Speed = x ÷ 3/4

Step 3

To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal (flip it!)

x ÷ 3/4 = x × 4/3 = 4x/3 miles per hour

Skill #4: Collecting Like Terms

What are "Like Terms"?

Like terms are the parts of an expression that have the same variable (letter).

  • 3x and 5x are like terms (both have x)
  • 2y and -7y are like terms (both have y)
  • 3x and 5y are NOT like terms (different letters)
  • Numbers without variables are also like terms with each other

The Process 🎯

Step 1

Circle or underline all the terms with the same variable

Step 2

If they have fractions, find a common denominator

Step 3

Add or subtract the numerators (keep the variable!)

Step 4

Simplify if possible

Example: 1/2k - 3/8k

Step 1

Both terms have "k" so they're like terms! ✓

Step 2

Find common denominator: 2 and 8 → Use 8

1/2 = 4/8 (multiply top and bottom by 4)

Step 3

Now subtract: 4/8k - 3/8k = 1/8k

Skill #5: Finding Common Denominators

Why Do We Need Common Denominators?

You can't add or subtract fractions unless they have the same denominator. It's like trying to add apples and oranges!

Two Methods:

Method 1: Multiply the Denominators

For 1/3 and 1/5:

  • Common denominator = 3 × 5 = 15
  • 1/3 = 5/15 (multiply top and bottom by 5)
  • 1/5 = 3/15 (multiply top and bottom by 3)

Method 2: Find the LCM (Least Common Multiple)

For 1/6 and 1/8:

  • Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30...
  • Multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24, 32...
  • LCM = 24
  • 1/6 = 4/24 and 1/8 = 3/24

Skill #6: Distributive Property

What is the Distributive Property?

When you multiply a number by a group in parentheses, you have to multiply it by EACH term inside.

a(b + c) = ab + ac

Think of it like distributing candy: If you have 3 bags with (2 chocolates + 5 gummies) in each, you have 3×2 chocolates and 3×5 gummies!

Example: 4(3x + 5)

Step 1

Multiply 4 by the first term: 4 × 3x = 12x

Step 2

Multiply 4 by the second term: 4 × 5 = 20

Step 3

Write the answer: 12x + 20

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • DON'T forget to distribute to BOTH terms
  • DON'T forget about negative signs
  • DON'T change the operation (+ or -) unless there's a negative outside

📝 Problem Set 1: Writing Expressions

Problem 1a: 5/7 m inches in feet

What This Problem Asks:

Convert 5/7 inches into feet. Remember: we're going from a SMALL unit (inches) to a BIG unit (feet).

Step 1

Remember the conversion: 12 inches = 1 foot

So each inch is 1/12 of a foot

Step 2

Set up the multiplication: 5/7 inches × 1/12

We multiply by 1/12 to convert inches to feet

Step 3

Multiply the fractions:

5/7 × 1/12 = (5×1)/(7×12) = 5/84 feet

Answer: 5/84 feet

💡 Why not divide? Because there are MORE inches than feet in the same distance. If something is 12 inches, it's only 1 foot. So we need to make our number smaller, which means multiplying by a fraction less than 1!

Problem 1b: Perimeter of a square with 2/3 inch sides

What This Problem Asks:

Find the total distance around a square where each side is 2/3 inch long.

Step 1

Remember: A square has 4 equal sides

Step 2

Perimeter formula for a square:

Perimeter = 4 × side
Step 3

Substitute and calculate:

Perimeter = 4 × 2/3 = 8/3 inches

Step 4

Convert to mixed number (optional):

8/3 = 2 2/3 inches

Answer: 8/3 inches or 2 2/3 inches

📐 Both forms are correct! The unit stays as inches because we never changed the unit.

Problem 1c: Number of pounds in 9 ounces

What This Problem Asks:

Convert 9 ounces to pounds. Again, small unit to big unit!

Step 1

Remember the conversion: 16 ounces = 1 pound

So each ounce is 1/16 of a pound

Step 2

Set up the multiplication:

9 ounces × 1/16 = 9/16 pounds

Answer: 9/16 pounds

⚖️ This fraction can't be simplified because 9 and 16 don't share any common factors!

Problem 1d: Average speed of a train

What This Problem Asks:

A train travels x miles in 3/4 hour. What's its speed?

Step 1

Remember the speed formula:

Speed = Distance ÷ Time
Step 2

Plug in the values:

Speed = x miles ÷ 3/4 hour

Step 3

To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal:

x ÷ 3/4 = x × 4/3 = 4x/3

Answer: 4x/3 miles per hour

🚂 This makes sense! If the train goes x miles in 3/4 hour, it would go even MORE miles in a full hour!

Problem 1e: Multi-Step Age Problem

What This Problem Asks:

Given relationships between ages, find Jill's age in terms of E (Eli's age).

  • Devin is 1 1/4 years younger than Eli
  • April is 1/5 as old as Devin
  • Jill is 5 years older than April
Step 1

Find Devin's age:

Devin = Eli - 1 1/4 = E - 5/4

(Convert 1 1/4 to improper fraction: 5/4)

Step 2

Find April's age:

April = 1/5 × Devin = 1/5(E - 5/4)

Distribute: = E/5 - 5/20 = E/5 - 1/4

Step 3

Find Jill's age:

Jill = April + 5

Jill = E/5 - 1/4 + 5

Step 4

Combine the constants:

Convert 5 to fourths: 5 = 20/4

Jill = E/5 - 1/4 + 20/4 = E/5 + 19/4

Answer: E/5 + 19/4 or E/5 + 4 3/4

👥 Key strategy: Work through ONE person at a time, writing each age in terms of E!

📝 Problem Set 2: Collecting Like Terms

Problem 2a: 1/2k - 3/8k

What This Problem Asks:

Combine the like terms (both have "k").

Step 1

Identify like terms: Both terms have "k" ✓

Step 2

Find common denominator: 2 and 8 → Use 8

1/2 = 4/8 (multiply top and bottom by 4)

Step 3

Subtract:

4/8k - 3/8k = 1/8k

Answer: 1/8k

Problem 2b: 2r/5 + 7r/15

Step 1

Identify like terms: Both terms have "r" ✓

Step 2

Find common denominator: 5 and 15 → Use 15

2r/5 = 6r/15 (multiply top and bottom by 3)

Step 3

Add:

6r/15 + 7r/15 = 13r/15

Answer: 13r/15

Problem 2c: -1/3a - 1/2b - 3/4 + 1/5b - 2/5b + 5/8a

What This Problem Asks:

This is a BIG one! We have three different types of terms: "a" terms, "b" terms, and constants (plain numbers).

Step 1

Group like terms:

a terms: -1/3a + 5/8a

b terms: -1/2b + 1/5b - 2/5b

Constants: -3/4

Step 2

Combine a terms:

Common denominator for 3 and 8 is 24

-1/3a = -8/24a

5/8a = 15/24a

-8/24a + 15/24a = 7/24a

Step 3

Combine b terms:

Common denominator for 2 and 5 is 10

-1/2b = -5/10b

1/5b - 2/5b = -1/5b = -2/10b

-5/10b - 2/10b = -7/10b

Step 4

Write the final answer:

7/24a - 7/10b - 3/4

Answer: 7/24a - 7/10b - 3/4

🎯 Pro tip: Work with ONE variable at a time to avoid confusion!

Problem 2d: -p + 3/5q - 1/10q + 1/5 - 1/5p + 2 1/2p

Step 1

Group like terms:

p terms: -p - 1/5p + 2 1/2p

q terms: 3/5q - 1/10q

Constants: 1/5

Step 2

Combine p terms:

Convert to fifths: -p = -5/5p, 2 1/2p = 5/2p = 12.5/5p

-5/5p - 1/5p + 12.5/5p = 6.5/5p = 13/10p

Wait, let's be more careful! 2 1/2 = 5/2

Common denominator is 10:

-10/10p - 2/10p + 25/10p = 13/10p

Step 3

Combine q terms:

3/5q - 1/10q = 6/10q - 1/10q = 5/10q = 1/2q

Answer: 13/10p + 1/2q + 1/5 (or 1.3p + 0.5q + 0.2)

Problem 2e: 5/7y - 1/14 - 5/7y + 3/14

Step 1

Notice something special!

We have +5/7y and -5/7y. These are opposites!

Step 2

Combine y terms:

5/7y - 5/7y = 0

The y terms cancel out completely!

Step 3

Combine constants:

-1/14 + 3/14 = 2/14 = 1/7

Answer: 1/7

✨ When opposites cancel, you're left with just the constants!

Problem 2f: 3m/8 - n/4 + 2n/2

Step 1

Simplify first:

2n/2 = n (anything divided by itself equals 1)

Step 2

Group like terms:

m terms: 3m/8 (only one, nothing to combine!)

n terms: -n/4 + n

Step 3

Combine n terms:

-n/4 + n = -n/4 + 4n/4 = 3n/4

Answer: 3m/8 + 3n/4

⚠️ m and n are different variables, so they can't be combined!

📝 Problem Set 3: Distributive Property

Problem 3a: 4/5(15x - 5)

What This Problem Asks:

Use the distributive property to multiply 4/5 by each term in the parentheses.

Step 1

Distribute 4/5 to the first term:

4/5 × 15x = (4×15)/(5×1) × x = 60/5 × x = 12x

Step 2

Distribute 4/5 to the second term:

4/5 × (-5) = -20/5 = -4

Step 3

Write the answer:

12x - 4

Answer: 12x - 4

Problem 3b: 4/5(4/5c - 5)

Step 1

Distribute 4/5 to the first term:

4/5 × 4/5c = 16/25c

Step 2

Distribute 4/5 to the second term:

4/5 × (-5) = -20/5 = -4

Answer: 16/25c - 4

Problem 3c: 2 4/5(3/4p + 1 1/5)

Step 1

Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions:

2 4/5 = 14/5

1 1/5 = 6/5

Step 2

Rewrite: 14/5(3/4p + 6/5)

Step 3

Distribute 14/5 to first term:

14/5 × 3/4p = 42/20p = 21/10p

Step 4

Distribute 14/5 to second term:

14/5 × 6/5 = 84/25

Answer: 21/10p + 84/25

Problem 3d: 8 - 4(1/6r - 3 1/2)

Step 1

Distribute -4 to each term in parentheses:

-4 × 1/6r = -4/6r = -2/3r

-4 × (-3 1/2) = -4 × (-7/2) = 28/2 = 14

Step 2

Write it out:

8 - 2/3r + 14

Step 3

Combine constants:

8 + 14 = 22

Answer: -2/3r + 22

⚠️ Remember: negative × negative = positive!

More Problems in Set 3

The homework has many more problems (3e through 3r). They all follow the same pattern:

  1. Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions
  2. Distribute (multiply) the outside number to EACH term inside
  3. Simplify each fraction
  4. Combine like terms if possible

1a. 5/7 m inches in feet

Answer: 5/84 feet

Steps:

  1. Remember: 12 inches = 1 foot, so 1 inch = 1/12 foot
  2. Multiply: 5/7 × 1/12
  3. = (5×1)/(7×12) = 5/84 feet

1b. Perimeter of square with 2/3 inch sides

Answer: 8/3 inches (or 2 2/3 inches)

Steps:

  1. Perimeter of square = 4 × side
  2. = 4 × 2/3
  3. = 8/3 inches = 2 2/3 inches

Answer: 9/16 pounds

Steps:

  1. Remember: 16 oz = 1 pound, so 1 oz = 1/16 pound
  2. 9 oz × 1/16 = 9/16 pounds

1d. Average speed: x miles in 3/4 hour

Answer: 4x/3 miles per hour

Steps:

  1. Speed = Distance ÷ Time
  2. = x ÷ 3/4
  3. = x × 4/3 (flip and multiply)
  4. = 4x/3 mph

1e. Jill's age in terms of E

Answer: E/5 + 19/4 (or E/5 + 4 3/4)

Steps:

  1. Devin = E - 1 1/4 = E - 5/4
  2. April = 1/5 × Devin = 1/5(E - 5/4) = E/5 - 1/4
  3. Jill = April + 5 = E/5 - 1/4 + 5
  4. = E/5 - 1/4 + 20/4 = E/5 + 19/4
