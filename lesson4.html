Lesson 4: Writing Products as Sums and Sums as Products

The Distributive Property & Factoring

📚 Understanding the Distributive Property

🍕 The Pizza Party Analogy

Imagine you're ordering pizza for 3 friends. Each friend wants 2 slices of pepperoni + 1 slice of cheese.

You could count it two ways:

  • Product form: 3 × (2 pepperoni + 1 cheese) = 3 friends each getting their combo
  • Sum form: 6 pepperoni + 3 cheese = counting all slices separately

Both describe the SAME amount of pizza! That's the distributive property!

The Distributive Property Formula

a(b + c) = ab + ac

Forward (Expanding): Multiply the outside number by EACH term inside the parentheses

Backward (Factoring): Find what's COMMON to all terms and pull it out

🔍 Finding the GCF (Greatest Common Factor)

To factor an expression:

  1. Find the GCF of all the numbers (coefficients)
  2. Find any common variables
  3. Divide each term by the GCF
  4. Write as: GCF(what's left)

Example: Factor 48j + 60k + 24

48
= 12 × 4
60
= 12 × 5
24
= 12 × 2

GCF = 12

48j + 60k + 24 = 12(4j + 5k + 2)

⚠️ Negatives & Opposites

Important Rule: The opposite of an expression means multiply by −1

Opposite of (−6v + 1) is −(−6v + 1) = 6v − 1

Distributing a negative:

−(a + b) = −a − b
−(a − b) = −a + b

The negative FLIPS the sign of every term inside!

💡 Memory Trick: "Distribute means DELIVER" — deliver the outside number to EVERY term inside, like a mail carrier delivering to every house on the street!

Example 1: Expanding (Product → Sum)

Expand: 2(x + 5)

1

Multiply 2 by the first term: 2 × x = 2x

2

Multiply 2 by the second term: 2 × 5 = 10

✓

Answer: 2(x + 5) = 2x + 10

Expand: 7(x − 3)

1

Multiply 7 by x: 7 × x = 7x

2

Multiply 7 by −3: 7 × (−3) = −21

✓

Answer: 7(x − 3) = 7x − 21

Example 2: Factoring (Sum → Product)

Factor: 72t + 8

1

Find GCF of 72 and 8: GCF = 8

2

Divide each term by 8:

72t ÷ 8 = 9t

8 ÷ 8 = 1

✓

Answer: 72t + 8 = 8(9t + 1)

Factor: 36z + 72

1

Find GCF of 36 and 72: GCF = 36

2

Divide each term:

36z ÷ 36 = z and 72 ÷ 36 = 2

✓

Answer: 36z + 72 = 36(z + 2)

Example 3: Repeated Addition → Product

Write as product: (x + 5) + (x + 5) + (x + 5)

1

Count how many times (x + 5) appears: 3 times

✓

Answer: 3(x + 5)

Check by expanding: 3x + 15

Original sum: x+5 + x+5 + x+5 = 3x + 15 ✓

Example 4: Distributing Negatives

Expand: −3(2p − 3q)

1

Multiply −3 by 2p: (−3)(2p) = −6p

2

Multiply −3 by −3q: (−3)(−3q) = +9q

(Negative × Negative = Positive!)

✓

Answer: −3(2p − 3q) = −6p + 9q

Simplify: −a − (a − b)

1

Distribute the negative to the parentheses:

−(a − b) = −a + b

2

Combine with the first −a:

−a + (−a + b) = −a − a + b = −2a + b

✓

Answer: −a − (a − b) = −2a + b

Mini Golf Problem

Expression 1 (Total for group):

6(2g + 20t − 3)

Expression 2 (Expanded):

12g + 120t − 18

Both represent the same total cost!

🎉 Key Takeaways:
  • Distributive Property: a(b + c) = ab + ac
  • Factoring is the REVERSE of distributing
  • Find the GCF first when factoring
  • Negative signs flip ALL terms inside parentheses
