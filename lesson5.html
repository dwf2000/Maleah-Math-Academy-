Lesson 5: Using Identity and Inverse to Write Equivalent Expressions

The Magic Numbers: 0 and 1, and Their Partners!

📚 Understanding Identity & Inverse Properties

🪞 The Mirror & Reset Button Analogy

Think of math operations like a video game:

  • Identity = The "do nothing" move. Adding 0 or multiplying by 1 leaves you exactly where you started!
  • Inverse = The "undo" button. It cancels out what you did and takes you back to the starting point (0 for addition, 1 for multiplication)!

🔑 The Four Magic Properties

Additive Identity

a + 0 = a
Adding zero changes nothing!
5 + 0 = 5

Multiplicative Identity

a × 1 = a
Multiplying by one changes nothing!
7 × 1 = 7

Additive Inverse

a + (−a) = 0
A number + its opposite = 0
5 + (−5) = 0

Multiplicative Inverse

a × (1/a) = 1
A number × its reciprocal = 1
5 × (1/5) = 1

📖 Key Vocabulary

Term Meaning Example
Opposite (Additive Inverse) The number that adds to give 0 Opposite of 6 is −6
Reciprocal (Multiplicative Inverse) The number that multiplies to give 1 Reciprocal of 7 is 1/7
Sum Result of addition Sum of 3 and 5 is 8
Product Result of multiplication Product of 3 and 5 is 15

🎯 Finding Opposites and Reciprocals

Finding the OPPOSITE (Additive Inverse)

Just change the sign!

  • Opposite of 6c → −6c
  • Opposite of −11 → 11
  • Opposite of (1 − 2g) → −(1 − 2g) = −1 + 2g

Finding the RECIPROCAL (Multiplicative Inverse)

Flip the fraction! (Or make it a fraction and flip)

  • Reciprocal of 7 → 1/7
  • Reciprocal of −5 → −1/5
  • Reciprocal of 1/4 → 4
  • Reciprocal of 3/4 → 4/3

🔄 Why This Matters: Simplifying Expressions!

💡 The Cancellation Power

When you add a number and its opposite, they CANCEL OUT to 0!

6c + (−6c) = 0

When you multiply a number by its reciprocal, they CANCEL OUT to 1!

(−2c)(−1/2) = 1c = c
💡 Pro Tip: When you see "the sum of A and the opposite of B," that's A + (−B), which equals A − B!
⚠️ Watch Out: The reciprocal of a NEGATIVE number is also NEGATIVE!

Reciprocal of −2 is −1/2 (NOT +1/2)

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Sum with Additive Inverse

Find: The sum of (6c − 5) and the opposite of 6c

1

Write the sum:

Opposite of 6c is −6c

(6c − 5) + (−6c)

2

Rewrite subtraction as addition:

6c + (−5) + (−6c)

3

Rearrange (Commutative Property):

6c + (−6c) + (−5)

4

Apply Additive Inverse: 6c + (−6c) = 0

0 + (−5)

✓

Apply Additive Identity: 0 + anything = anything

= −5

Example 2: Product with Multiplicative Inverse

Find: The product of (−2c + 14) and the multiplicative inverse of −2

1

Write the product:

Multiplicative inverse of −2 is −1/2

(−2c + 14)(−1/2)

2

Distribute:

(−2c)(−1/2) + (14)(−1/2)

3

Multiply each term:

(−2c)(−1/2) = 1c = c    (because −2 × −1/2 = 1)

(14)(−1/2) = −7

✓

Answer: c − 7

Example 3: Sum Problems (Problem 2 style)

a. Find: 6 and p − 6

6 + (p − 6) = 6 + p − 6 = p + 6 − 6 = p + 0 = p

The 6 and −6 cancel out!

b. Find: 10w + 3 and −3

(10w + 3) + (−3) = 10w + 3 − 3 = 10w + 0 = 10w

c. Find: −x − 11 and the opposite of −11

Opposite of −11 is 11

(−x − 11) + 11 = −x − 11 + 11 = −x + 0 = −x

d. Find: The opposite of 4x and 3 + 4x

Opposite of 4x is −4x

(−4x) + (3 + 4x) = −4x + 3 + 4x = 3 + (−4x + 4x) = 3 + 0 = 3

e. Find: 2g and the opposite of (1 − 2g)

Opposite of (1 − 2g) is −(1 − 2g) = −1 + 2g

2g + (−1 + 2g) = 2g − 1 + 2g = 4g − 1

Example 4: Product Problems (Problem 3 style)

a. Find: (7h − 1) and the multiplicative inverse of 7

Multiplicative inverse of 7 is 1/7

(7h − 1)(1/7) = (7h)(1/7) − (1)(1/7) = h − 1/7

b. Find: The multiplicative inverse of −5 and (10v − 5)

Multiplicative inverse of −5 is −1/5

(−1/5)(10v − 5) = (−1/5)(10v) − (−1/5)(5)

= −2v − (−1) = −2v + 1

Example 5: Standard Form (Problem 4 style)

Simplify: 1/4(4x + 8)

Distribute 1/4 to each term:

(1/4)(4x) + (1/4)(8) = x + 2

Simplify: 3/4(5x − 1)

Distribute 3/4 to each term:

(3/4)(5x) − (3/4)(1) = 15x/4 − 3/4

Or as mixed numbers: 3¾x − ¾

Quick Reference: The Four Properties

Property Formula What It Does
Additive Identity a + 0 = a Adding 0 changes nothing
Multiplicative Identity a × 1 = a Multiplying by 1 changes nothing
Additive Inverse a + (−a) = 0 Number + opposite = 0
Multiplicative Inverse a × (1/a) = 1 Number × reciprocal = 1
🎉 Key Takeaways:
  • Opposite = change the sign (used for addition/subtraction)
  • Reciprocal = flip the fraction (used for multiplication/division)
  • When things cancel out → you get 0 (addition) or 1 (multiplication)
  • Identity properties let you remove 0 or 1 from expressions
